# Version C — English with confidence

## Positioning
General English for Hungarians in Budapest, at every level. Lead with the learner's everyday goal; Cambridge/Clare College supports trust. Accent coaching, editing and Hungarian-to-English translation are supporting services.

## Visual system
- Body: Poppins Regular; emphasis: Poppins SemiBold. Headings: Oswald 600.
- Ink #172020 and ivory #faf8f2 provide readable contrast.
- Cyan #00dfe5, magenta #f000ca, lime #88ee15 and blush #f38aaf reference the supplied cinema image. Use the bright colours for accents; keep long text dark.
- Retro café illustration supplies atmosphere. The original supplied social profile image appears beside contact links for recognition. Neither image is presented as Mark's portrait.
- Keep QR modules dark on white with a four-module clear border. Put artwork around the code, never across it.

## Conversion path
Sticker or card → Hungarian-first page → practical skills → Cambridge credibility → email or SMS. The English switch offers the same information. A mobile contact button stays available while scrolling.

The offer is personal, face-to-face English tuition tailored to the learner's goals. Coffee is a meeting context, not an advertised free lesson. No unconfirmed prices, testimonials, guarantees or availability claims are used.

## Campaign plan
1. Place small, equal batches of B and C materials in comparable locations with the venue's permission. Keep placement dates and quantities in a simple log.
2. C sticker points to https://language-snippets.github.io/english-help-c/?source=sticker. C business card points to https://language-snippets.github.io/english-help-c/?source=card.
3. Email subjects and SMS drafts include C and the source. Record enquiries, suitable enquiries, meetings and booked lessons manually. Ask callers how they found Mark.
4. Compare outcomes after both versions have had similar exposure. These links do not count scans and there is no randomised traffic allocation. Treat the result as a small campaign comparison, not a controlled experiment.
5. Improve the weaker step: few enquiries suggests changing the hook or placement; many unsuitable enquiries suggests clarifying audience or lesson format. Publish rates or a specific introductory offer only after Mark decides them.
